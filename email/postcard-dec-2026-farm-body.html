Downtown Lights Up This Saturday

Santa, carolers, a sledding hill and late-night shopping on Avenida Del Mar.

By Jacob Hazzard
The Pier Bowl at dusk.

Hi %contact_first_name%,

The holidays are officially here, and this Saturday downtown San Clemente puts on its best night of the year. Santa’s Village by the Sea and Puttin’ on the Glitz take over Avenida Del Mar from 4 to 9 p.m., with Santa arriving on the street between 4 and 4:30. Our office is right in the middle of it, and I’ll have hot chocolate and treats out front (more below).

The Neighborhood
My wife, me and Hazel.
A lot of you know me from a knock on the door, so here’s a little more. I grew up in Forster Ranch and I’m an Eagle Scout. And please meet the newest addition to the Hazzard family: Hazel, our mini dachshund pup.

Come say hi this Saturday, Dec. 5. During Santa’s Village and Puttin’ on the Glitz, I’ll be out in front of our office, Luxre Realty at 222 Avenida Del Mar (the one with the putting green), handing out hot chocolate, cookies and candy canes, with giveaways and some sweet prizes. Bring the kids, take a putt, and say hello.
Around Town

Saturday, Dec. 5. Santa’s Village by the Sea and Puttin’ on the Glitz, 4–9 p.m., Avenida Del Mar and the Community Center (100 N. Calle Seville). Free to attend. The street is closed to cars, so park free at the Outlets and ride the free SC Trolley.

Saturday, Dec. 5. Teddy Bear Christmas Tea at Casa Romantica, 2:30 p.m., with carols and Mrs. Claus reading The Night Before Christmas. $85 per guest ($70 for members).

Dec. 11–13. Dana Point Boat Parade of Lights, 6:30–8 p.m. Free to watch from the harbor boardwalk. This year’s theme is “Harboritaville.” Get there early; parking fills up.
